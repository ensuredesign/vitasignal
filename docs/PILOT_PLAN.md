# VitaSignal AI — 90-Day Supervised Pilot Plan

## Pilot Objective
To prospectively evaluate the usability, clinical safety agreement, and patient acceptability of VitaSignal AI in a supervised primary care or fertility clinic setting, subject to regulatory and institutional approvals.

---

## Milestone Timeline

### Phase 1: Clinical & Regulatory Advisory (Days 0–30)
- Engage urologist, reproductive endocrinologist, and primary care advisory panel.
- Conduct expert review of decision rules and Arabic medical translation.
- Determine regulatory pathway (software as a medical device classification under UAE DoH / international standards).

### Phase 2: Supervised Usability & Synthetic Trial (Days 31–60)
- Execute simulated patient journey walkthroughs with 30 clinicians.
- Validate EHR interoperability via FHIR standards (`QuestionnaireResponse`, `Observation`, `ServiceRequest`).
- Complete comprehensive third-party cybersecurity and privacy impact audit (including ADHICS V2 readiness).

### Phase 3: Prospective Supervised Pilot (Days 61–90)
- Seek IRB / Ethics approval for a prospective pilot with a partner hospital or fertility clinic network.
- Measure:
  1. Intake completion rate.
  2. Time from symptom recognition to specialist evaluation.
  3. False escalation rate and clinical rule agreement.
  4. Patient trust, privacy satisfaction, and linguistic clarity.
