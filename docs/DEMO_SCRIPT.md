# VitaSignal AI — 3-Minute Judge Demonstration Script

## Act 1: The Preventive Gap (0:00 – 0:30)
- **Message:** In male reproductive health, care is frequently delayed due to stigma, privacy concerns, and friction. Men often don't know where to seek guidance.
- **Action:** Open landing page (`/`). Point out the clear synthetic demo banner, bilingual toggle (English / العربية), and the core promise: *Signal → Evidence → Action → Closure*.

---

## Act 2: SignalSense & EvidenceLens (0:30 – 1:30)
- **Scenario:** Launch Scenario **`D02`** from the Demo Scenarios page (`/demo`).
- **SignalSense Intake:** Show the safety gate first. Advance to anatomical history (prior testicular trauma).
- **EvidenceLens Extraction:** Open the synthetic semen analysis report. Click *Extract Observations with Gemini* (or *Use Verified Replay Fixture*). Highlight the verbatim evidence quote grounding and user candidate confirmation.

---

## Act 3: CarePath & Evidence Drawer (1:30 – 2:00)
- **Action:** Generate the Care Pathway.
- **Result:** Result directs to **Urology / Andrology Specialist Evaluation** (`SPEC-01`).
- **Explainability:** Click the *Evidence [C2]* button. Open the slide-out Evidence Drawer showing the AUA/ASRM clinical guideline citation and publisher details.

---

## Act 4: Emergency Gate Override (2:00 – 2:20)
- **Scenario:** Launch Scenario **`D04`** (Sudden severe testicular pain).
- **Demonstration:** Show that acute symptoms immediately bypass routine questionnaires, disable AI extraction, and trigger an emergency triage directive to seek immediate surgical evaluation.

---

## Act 5: PrivacyVault & CareLoop Closure (2:20 – 3:00)
- **Action:** Go to PrivacyVault (`/care`). Show that no fields are checked by default. Select only the confirmed urological history.
- **Snapshot:** Create an immutable 24-hour snapshot.
- **CareLoop:** Switch to the Care Coordinator panel (`/review`). Demonstrate state transitions (*Shared → Acknowledged → Outreach Simulated → Completed*).
- **Revoke:** Click *Revoke Share*. Show that coordinator access is instantly severed.
- **Evaluation:** Visit `/evaluation` to show actual benchmark test counts and metrics.
